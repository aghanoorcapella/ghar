# Ghar

A family life simulator for Noor, Tozia, Mir, Umair and Ariha. Play it in the browser: https://aghanoorcapella.github.io/ghar/

Karachi (Clifton Block 2), the haveli in Sultan Kot, Shikarpur, and Des Moines, Iowa, 11 hours apart. Nine minigames, daily events, achievements, and a reunion ending. Progress saves in your browser.
